About Percy

I am a fourth-year PhD candidate in the Mediterranean Art and Archaeology Program at the University of Virginia. I am studying under the tutelage of Dr. Tyler Jo Smith and my research concerns the Late Bronze Age and Early Iron Age on Crete.

My dissertation topic focuses on an understudied building on the southern outskirts of the archaeological site of Knossos. The original excavators of Knossos named the structure “Caravanserai” and deemed it a sort of guest house for travellers heading toward the main site. By reinvestigating the finds and structure of the Caravanserai, I hope to further illuminate the nature of its function throughout its lifespan.

Before arriving at UVA, I acquired my MA in Classics from Tulane University under the direction of Dr. Emilia Oddo. Through the generosity of Tulane and the British School at Athens, I was able to first set foot inside of the Knossos Stratigraphical Museum in the summer of 2022. There I found my passion for archival investigation and pottery analysis. Since that summer, I continued within the vein of archival archaeology and eventually acquired a permit to study the Caravanserai material.
